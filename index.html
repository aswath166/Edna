Final Year Capstone Project · Evaluation & Methodology Dossier

1. Mathematical & Ecological Formulations

Shannon-Wiener Entropy ($H'$)

$$H' = -\sum_{i=1}^{S} p_i \ln(p_i)$$

Measures community uncertainty and information content.
Gini-Simpson Diversity ($1 - D$)

$$1 - D = 1 - \frac{\sum n_i(n_i - 1)}{N(N - 1)}$$

Probability that two randomly drawn reads belong to distinct taxa.
Chao1 Richness Estimator ($S_{Chao1}$)

$$S_{Chao1} = S_{obs} + \frac{f_1^2}{2f_2}$$

Asymptotic richness accounting for unseen rare singletons ($f_1$) and doubletons ($f_2$).
Pielou's Evenness ($J'$)

$$J' = \frac{H'}{\ln(S_{obs})}$$

Equitability of species abundance distribution bounded in $[0, 1]$.
Bray-Curtis Dissimilarity ($BC$)

$$BC_{jk} = 1 - \frac{2\sum \min(c_{ji}, c_{ki})}{\sum c_{ji} + \sum c_{ki}}$$

Ecological community turnover between two geographic sites or timepoints.

2. Experimental Methodology & Pipeline Architecture

  1. Strand-Invariant Feature Representation: Reads are converted into canonical $k$-mer frequency vectors ($k=4$, 136-dimensional simplex) invariant under reverse-complement strand flips, then projected with $L_2$-norm normalization.
  2. Density-Based Operational Taxonomic Unit Clustering: Scikit-learn HDBSCAN partitions continuous manifold regions without assuming spherical Gaussian clusters or a pre-determined cluster count $K$.
  3. Multi-Tiered Divergence Categorization: Centroid distance thresholds ($\theta_{known}=0.35$, $\theta_{sister}=0.65$) and GLOSH outlier scores categorize reads into confirmed knowns, sister lineages, candidate novel taxa, or chimeric sequencing artifacts.
  4. Comprehensive Diversity Accounting: Unlike alignment pipelines which discard unaligned reads and artificially deflate diversity indices, BioEmbed incorporates unassigned dark clades into unified community metrics.
